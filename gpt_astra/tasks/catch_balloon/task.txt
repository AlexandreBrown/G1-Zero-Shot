Catch the red balloon moving in front of the robot. In simulation, a red sphere
represents the balloon. Track it in the ego camera and choose a reach only when
it is visible and within reach. Hold when unsure or when the target is not visible.
The camera cannot see behind the robot; never assume that unseen space is clear.
The available actions are bounded arm reaches with stationary feet; they do not
close fingers or provide a guaranteed grasp.
