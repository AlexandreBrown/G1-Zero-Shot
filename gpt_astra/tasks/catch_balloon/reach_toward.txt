Reach toward the red balloon whenever you can see it. In simulation, a red sphere
represents the balloon.
For this task the balloon counts as within reach whenever any part of it is visible
in the ego camera: on the floor, low, far away, partly hidden or at the edge of the
image. The reach does not have to touch it; it points the arms toward it.
Choose the arm by where the balloon is in the image: reach_left when it is in the
left part of the image (the robot's left), reach_right when it is in the right part,
reach_both when it is near the middle.
Hold only when no red balloon is visible, or when a person's body is within arm's
length in front of the robot, where a reach could hit them.
The camera cannot see behind the robot; never assume that unseen space is clear.
The available actions are bounded arm reaches with stationary feet; they do not
close fingers or provide a guaranteed grasp.
